EXPERI/MENTS

Things made to find something out. A few live on this site; most live in my notes. They are allowed to be unfinished, weird, or wrong.

+ live  * drafting  ~ ongoing  . shelved  x failed. Nothing here claims to have caused a breakthrough. Every entry exists because asking about it was worth something.

+ #001

ASCII field

The portrait on the front page. Luminance sampled from a photograph, mapped to characters on a canvas, drawn in slowly shifting strips. The field is the method; the page is the field.

~ #003

Soft reboot

A test of what a quiet default looks like: no analytics, no tracking, no autoplay sound, no begging for attention. This site is the experiment — one you can check in DevTools: the only network requests it ever makes are to itself, and every font it loads lives in this repo.

* #004

Prompts as tools

Treating a prompt as an engineering input — constraints, precision, versioning. The last round of edits to this site was described in plain sentences, reviewed as a diff, and shipped with a single command. Learned the output is only as honest as the edges drawn around it. Notes kept; still drafting the ones that work.

~ #005

Linux side rooms

A growing interest in systems without the cruft — small machines, terminals, configs where everything visible is there on purpose. Where the machine answers directly: the portrait on the front page is rotated, measured, and sampled with command-line tools alone — ImageMagick, Python, curl — nowhere near a web app.

First words in Japanese

Learning a language is the most honest systems project there is: every new word is a component shipped without compatibility docs. Still near the start, on purpose.
